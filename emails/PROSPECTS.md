## Working rules

**Recreate drafts, never update them.** `update_draft` detaches a draft from
its thread — it returns a fresh threadId, so the reply would go out as a brand
new conversation. Delete and re-create with `replyToMessageId`.

**Never offer maintenance** — not in any wording, not as a favour, not as
"free for the life of the site". Content that changes goes into a plain text
file the client edits themselves on GitHub, and the page loads from it. Full
wording and caveats in ARAK.txt; the first working example is
`sminktetovalas-tatabanya/`.

**Watch the typos.** The Tisztítlak reply went out with "Kedves Tisztillak",
"ugényelt" and "adatvédlem". At 200 mails a day, a typo in the greeting is the
one people notice.
